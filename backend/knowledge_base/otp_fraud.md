# OTP Fraud

## Description
OTP Fraud occurs when criminals trick users into sharing One-Time Passwords to gain unauthorized access to bank accounts, UPI accounts, or online services.

## Common Signs
- Someone asks for your OTP.
- Calls claiming to be from a bank.
- Fake customer support.
- Prize or lottery scams.
- Urgent verification requests.

## Immediate Steps
1. Never share your OTP.
2. Contact your bank if OTP was shared.
3. Block affected accounts if necessary.
4. Change passwords.
5. Monitor bank transactions.

## Prevention
- Never disclose OTPs.
- Verify unknown callers.
- Enable transaction alerts.
- Use official customer support numbers.
