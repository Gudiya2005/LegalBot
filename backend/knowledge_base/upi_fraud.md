# UPI Fraud

## Description
UPI Fraud involves unauthorized financial transactions through UPI applications like Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM.

## Common Signs
- Unexpected payment requests.
- Fake QR codes.
- Fraudulent payment links.
- Unknown UPI transactions.
- Fake customer care numbers.

## Immediate Steps
1. Contact your bank immediately.
2. Freeze your account if required.
3. Report the transaction.
4. Save transaction details.
5. Call 1930 immediately.

## Prevention
- Never approve unknown payment requests.
- Verify QR codes.
- Avoid fake customer care numbers.
- Enable app security.
