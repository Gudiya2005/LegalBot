# Digital Arrest Scam

## Description
Digital Arrest is a scam where criminals pretend to be police officers, CBI, ED, or government officials and falsely accuse victims of crimes to extort money.

## Common Signs
- Video calls from fake officials.
- Threats of arrest.
- Demands for immediate payment.
- Fake legal documents.
- Pressure to stay on continuous calls.

## Immediate Steps
1. End the call immediately.
2. Do not transfer money.
3. Verify with official authorities.
4. Save call recordings if available.
5. Report the incident immediately.

## Prevention
- Government agencies do not demand money over calls.
- Verify official identities.
- Never share banking information.
- Stay calm during threatening calls.
