# Phishing

## Description
Phishing is a cybercrime where attackers trick users into revealing sensitive information such as passwords, OTPs, bank details, or personal information through fake emails, messages, websites, or phone calls.

## Common Signs
- Suspicious emails or SMS
- Fake login pages
- Urgent requests for personal information
- Unknown links
- Offers that seem too good to be true

## Immediate Steps
1. Do not click suspicious links.
2. Close the fake website immediately.
3. Change passwords if credentials were entered.
4. Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).
5. Scan your device for malware.

## Prevention
- Verify the sender before responding.
- Check website URLs carefully.
- Never share passwords or OTPs.
- Keep software updated.
- Use antivirus software.
