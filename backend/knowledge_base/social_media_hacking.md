# Social Media Account Hacking

## Description
Social media account hacking occurs when an unauthorized person gains access to a user's social media account without permission.

## Common Signs
- Password changed unexpectedly
- Unable to log in
- Unknown posts or messages
- Email or phone number changed
- Unknown login alerts

## Immediate Steps
1. Reset your password immediately.
2. Recover your account using the platform's recovery option.
3. Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).
4. Inform your contacts.
5. Check connected devices and log out of unknown sessions.

## Prevention
- Use strong passwords.
- Enable 2FA.
- Avoid phishing links.
- Never share OTPs.
- Keep recovery email updated.
